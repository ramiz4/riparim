# Workshop guides and social drafts

The public guide index and two articles live in the typed
[guide messages](../../lib/i18n/guide-messages.ts). The small
[route registry](../../lib/guide-routes.ts) owns their public URL identities.
They use existing catalogue filters, direct workshop contact and moderated review
submission. They do not promise booking, repair quality, prices or legal rights.

The articles are original Riparim workflow guidance. General questions about
workshop experience, communication and an itemized estimate were checked against
[AAA](https://www.aaa.com/autorepair/articles/questions-to-ask-when-picking-an-auto-repair-shop)
and [ADAC](https://www.adac.de/rund-ums-fahrzeug/reparatur-pflege-wartung/werkstatt-tipps/autowerkstatt-auftrag-kosten-termine/).
The pages link to both as further reading. Foreign consumer-law rules, price
limits and contractual guarantees are deliberately outside this guidance.

## Draft: choosing a workshop

Use only after the corresponding verified main release is live. These are drafts
for manual selection and review; no account, campaign or recurring publication
is configured. Replace nothing in profile or catalogue data when sharing.

- **DE:** Welche Werkstatt passt zu deinem Anliegen? Vergleiche Leistungen,
  Standort und Bewertungsquellen und kläre offene Fragen direkt. Unsere kurze
  Checkliste hilft dir beim nächsten Schritt:
  https://riparim.com/ratgeber/werkstatt-auswaehlen
- **SQ:** Cili servis i përshtatet nevojës tënde? Krahaso shërbimet, vendndodhjen
  dhe burimet e vlerësimeve dhe sqaro pyetjet drejtpërdrejt. Lista jonë e shkurtër
  të ndihmon me hapin tjetër:
  https://riparim.com/sq/udhezues/zgjedhja-e-servisit
- **EN:** Which workshop fits your job? Compare services, location and review
  sources, then ask the workshop directly. Our short checklist helps you take
  the next step:
  https://riparim.com/en/guides/choosing-a-workshop

## Draft: preparing a visit

- **DE:** Vor dem Werkstattbesuch: Beobachtungen notieren, Untersuchungskosten
  erfragen und Rückfragen vor Zusatzarbeiten vereinbaren. Hier findest du die
  Checkliste für ein klares Gespräch:
  https://riparim.com/ratgeber/werkstattbesuch-vorbereiten
- **SQ:** Para vizitës në servis: shëno vërejtjet, pyet për kostot e kontrollit
  dhe dakordohu për kontakt para punëve shtesë. Këtu e gjen listën për një bisedë
  të qartë:
  https://riparim.com/sq/udhezues/pergatitja-e-vizites
- **EN:** Before visiting a workshop: note your observations, ask about inspection
  costs and agree on contact before additional work. Use this checklist for a
  clearer conversation:
  https://riparim.com/en/guides/preparing-a-visit

Measure visits and contact actions using the existing approved analytics scope.
A contact click does not establish a message, visit or completed repair.
